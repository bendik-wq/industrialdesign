How to film long-form that clips

Rules for every video

  1. One chapter answers one buyer question, in 45–90 seconds.
  2. Open every chapter with a hook line, as if it were the first 3 seconds of a Reel.
  3. Never say “like I said earlier.” Each chapter has to make sense on its own.
  4. One concrete number or deal example per chapter.
  5. Put chapter titles on screen and in the YouTube description.
  6. Talk strategy to owners, not beginner tips. Owners want big ideas and structure.
  7. Pull topics from the topic bank: real questions from calls, DMs and applications.
  8. End every video with the same call to action: watch the VSL, apply.

Rules

$1M+ or no call

Asked in the DM and on the application. Below $1M, no sales conversation.

YouTube is the engine

Long-form does the selling. Reels exist to send people to the full video.

Film for the clip

Every long-form video is built from standalone chapters, so clipping is easy.

micro1 is separate

Only large companies, by revenue or headcount.

Pricing

$20k is a fair launch price, but low for this buyer. Take the first 3–5 clients at $20k for case studies. Then move to $25k upfront plus a success fee when the deal closes.

Make the guarantee conditional: no signed LOI by day 90, you keep working at no cost. Check broker and finder rules before charging a fee tied to closing.
