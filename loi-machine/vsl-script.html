Hook

If your business does more than a million dollars a year, this is the fastest way I know to double it.

Not by hiring more salespeople. Not by running more ads. By buying another business like yours.

And in this video I’m going to show you how we get owners like you a signed letter of intent on a seven-figure acquisition in 90 days, without putting your own money into the deal.

The seller finances part of it. The bank finances the rest. And we structure it so the financing covers more than the purchase price, so you’re not writing a check to close.

0:45
Who it’s for

Quick filter before we go any further. This is only for owners whose business already does a million dollars or more a year.

That’s not me being picky. It’s how the math works. Lenders and sellers look at your business before they look at the deal. Your revenue, your profit and your track record are the collateral. Under a million, the structure I’m about to show you usually doesn’t get funded.

If that’s you, stay. If not, the videos on our channel will help you get there, and we’ll be here when you do.

1:30
The ceiling

Here’s what I hear from almost every owner at your stage.

You built something real. Seven figures. Customers, a team, systems that mostly work.

But the next million is harder than the first one. Growth is 10, maybe 20 percent a year. Every new hire is a risk. Every new channel takes a year to figure out. And you’re still the person everything runs through.

At that pace, doubling takes you four, five, six years. If nothing goes wrong.

2:30
Three ways

There are really only two ways most owners try to grow from here.

The first is to grind it out organically. More marketing, more staff, more hours. It works, but it’s slow, and it’s all on you.

The second is to raise money. Bring in a partner or private equity. That’s faster, but you give up equity and control of the company you built.

There’s a third way that most owners never seriously consider, because they think it needs a pile of cash.

You buy growth. You acquire a business that already has the customers, the team and the cash flow. And the day you close, your revenue jumps.

Buy one business your size and you’ve doubled. Buy two and you’ve tripled. That’s how the big companies in your industry got big. They didn’t out-market everyone. They bought them.

3:45
Why owners don’t

So why isn’t every seven-figure owner doing this? Three beliefs.

One: “I need a lot of cash.” You don’t, if the deal is structured right. I’ll show you the structure in a minute.

Two: “The bank will say no.” Banks don’t reject buyers. They reject structures. Bring them the right one and they want the deal.

Three: “Good businesses never come up for sale.” Most of them never get listed. Thousands of owners are approaching retirement with no succession plan. They don’t want an auction. They want to hand their business to someone they trust. If you go to them directly, you’re often the only buyer in the conversation.

5:00
The structure

Let me show you how a no-money-down deal actually works. Here’s a simple example.

Say you find a business priced at two and a half million.

Two pieces make it work.

Vendor financing. The seller agrees to be paid part of the price over time, out of the business’s profits. They do it because it gets them a better price, a tax advantage, and a buyer who actually knows how to run a company.

Overfinancing. We don’t just finance the purchase price. We structure the debt to also cover closing costs and working capital. So you close without writing a check, and the business has cash in the bank from day one.

And because you already run a seven-figure business, you’re exactly who lenders and sellers want to back. Your experience is the down payment.

7:15
The 90 days

Here’s exactly how we get you to a signed LOI in 90 days.

Days 1 to 30: buy box and deal flow. We define what you should buy: industry, size, location, and how it fits your business. Then we build the target list and start reaching out to owners directly.

Days 31 to 60: owner conversations and valuation. You get on calls with real sellers. We pull the numbers, value the best targets, and start testing the financing with lenders.

Days 61 to 90: structure and sign. We build the vendor financing and overfinancing into the offer, negotiate it with you, and get a seven-figure LOI signed.

You don’t do this alone and you don’t learn it from a course. We work the deal with you.

Not for you if

This isn’t for everyone, so let me be clear.

It’s not for you if your business does under a million a year. It’s not for you if you want someone to hand you a business with no work on your part. You’ll be on owner calls. You’ll make decisions.

And it’s not for you if you’re “just curious.” We take on a small number of owners at a time because we work every deal personally.

Cost of waiting

Think about what it costs to keep doing it the slow way.

Another four or five years to double. Your competitors buying the businesses you could have bought. Retiring owners selling to private equity because nobody else showed up.

The businesses that will make you twice as big already exist today. The only question is who buys them.
