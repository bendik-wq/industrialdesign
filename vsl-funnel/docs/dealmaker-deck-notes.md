### Frameworks and method (Josh's teaching; usable as method once he is happy with the wording)
- Three rungs: business owner, business developer, dealmaker (27 to 32); operator vs acquirer (26).
- Buy vs build: opening a new location costs $200K to $1M before day one (36); a business pays three ways: cash at closing,
  cash flow, equity (40); roll-up vs conglomerate (43, 44); two endings: hand it down, or sell (45); four levers (47).
- Multiple arbitrage: buy small at about 4x, a portfolio is worth 8 to 12x (41). The script already uses 3 to 5x and
  8 to 12x as illustrative ranges.
- Credibility by board: recruit a board with "free founder's equity" (61 to 66), with the outreach message (63).
- Capital: three mechanisms (vendor finance, commercial lending, vendor rollover) with timing claims of two to four weeks
  and six to sixteen weeks (68); debt not investors (70); two rules (71); four worked structures on a $1M revenue, $250K
  EBITDA business: 100% vendor finance, interest-only with balloons, 60/40 bank and rollover, 70/20/10 stacked (72 to 76).
- Closing: six steps (sourcing, offers at 3 to 5x EBITDA, due diligence, financing, legals, post-closing) (80); target
  profile $500K+ revenue, 15%+ margins, owner wants out (81); three documents before an offer and quality of earnings
  (82); what a lender asks: "what guarantees our money back?" (83).
- A temperament slide quoting a coach who says he coaches four billionaires (56): third-party claim, leave out.
