# Data page VSL: "Get paid for data your business already creates"

**Presenter:** Josh Li, to camera. **Length:** about 3½ minutes (about 520 words).
**Built with:** Sultanic frameworks: X opener → market has moved → copy payoff sequence → not-your-fault → timeline → assumptive questions → emotional spending → after-close.
**Goal:** one action, which is to click "Claim my data valuation" under the video.
**Rules:** no fake deals and no guaranteed numbers. Say "up to" and "typically". Disclose the referral fee.

---

// X OPENER

This isn't for everyone.

If you started your business last year, click away. This won't apply to you.

But if you've run a real business for a few years, with real jobs, real processes and real records, you could get paid up to two million dollars for that data. Even if that business has since closed. And odds are nobody has ever told you.

// MARKET HAS MOVED

Here's what's changed.

The AI companies have largely run through the public internet: the websites, the books, the forums. What they don't have, and badly need, is data on how real businesses actually run. How a job gets scheduled. How a part gets ordered. How a problem gets fixed.

That data doesn't sit on the internet. It sits inside businesses like yours.

Our partner, [our partner], recently had [FILL: confirmed figure, e.g. $1 billion] committed to buying it. Deals typically land between two hundred thousand and two million dollars.

// COPY PAYOFF SEQUENCE

So in the next three minutes, I'm going to show you how to turn data you're already creating into a cash payment that can recapitalise your business, pay down your debt, or fund your next product. All without bringing in an investor, selling a single share or signing another loan.

Once you have that cash, you get to strengthen the balance sheet on your terms, and you never have to hand over a slice of the company to do it.

And when the expensive debt is gone, the repayments come off your cash flow every single month. That frees you from the pressure that comes with every bank statement.

// NOT-YOUR-FAULT FRAME

Now, if nobody's offered you this before, it has nothing to do with how well you run the business, or your industry, or your size.

No. It has everything to do with timing. Until recently, nobody was buying this. Now somebody is, with a billion dollars behind them.

// OBJECTION FLIP

And your first thought is probably "I'm not selling my customers' details." Good, because they don't want them.

Your customers' names, numbers and card details stay with you. They buy operational data: the workflows, the job history, the scheduling, the maintenance logs. All of it anonymised.

No equity. No control. Nothing changes on Monday morning.

// THE CLOSED-BUSINESS TWIST (belief shift: "that chapter's over")

And here's the part that surprises people.

It doesn't have to be a business you're running today.

If you closed a business, or sold it and kept the records, those years of job history, scheduling and operations didn't disappear when the doors shut. To a buyer like our partner, that history is still valuable. You might have something worth up to two million dollars sitting in an old hard drive or a cloud account you haven't opened in years.

So if that's you, pick "Closed down" or "Sold" on the form. It still counts.

(If the company went through liquidation, the records may belong to the liquidator. We'll help you check.)

// TIMELINE LANGUAGE

First, you claim your valuation. It takes about a minute, right under this video.

Then our partner looks at what you have and how much of it, and puts a number on it.

Once you see the number, you decide. If it works, you sell. If it doesn't, you walk away. It costs you nothing either way.

// ASSUMPTIVE QUESTIONS

And the reason the numbers are this big right now is that the budget is being deployed now. The businesses that move first get the premium prices. Once it's allocated, it's allocated.

And that's why we're doing this. In the interest of full transparency, we may receive a referral fee from our partner. It doesn't come out of what you're paid.

// EMOTIONAL SPENDING CLOSE

So it's not really a question of whether your data is worth something. It's a question of what you're going to do with it.

You can recapitalise, and put the business on the strongest footing it's ever had without giving away a single share.

You can pay down the bad debt, and wake up without the repayments hanging over you.

You can launch the product, the location or the hire you've been putting off for two years.

Or you can finally pay yourself, after years of putting everything back in.

The choice is yours. But the first choice is finding out your number.

// AFTER-CLOSE + CTA

Thirty days from now, one of two things will be true. Either your data is still sitting on a hard drive, doing nothing. Or you know exactly what it's worth, and maybe it's already working for you.

The only difference is the next sixty seconds.

Click "Claim my data valuation" right under this video, and we'll take you straight to our partner to get your number.

## Recording notes
- **Open tight:** start cold on "This isn't for everyone." The first 5 seconds decide the play rate.
- **On-screen text, at the moments it's said:**
  - "Up to $2,000,000"
  - "Recapitalise · Pay down debt · Fund what's next"
  - "Not customer data"
  - "No equity · no loan"
  - "Claim my data valuation ↓"
- **Burn in captions.** Most people will watch on mute first.
- **Before recording:** confirm the partner name and committed figure in writing.
- **Hosting:** upload to YouTube (unlisted), Vimeo or Loom. Paste the link into Funnel HQ → Integrations → Data → "Data page video".
- **Ads:** run each ad cut to the matching headline. For example, the "pay down debt" ad goes to `https://data.grosfjeld-li.com/debt`, so the ad and the page say the same thing. Every headline has its own link like this (`/recap`, `/revenue`, …), and those visitors count towards that headline.
