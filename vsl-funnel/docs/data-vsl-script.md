**Presenter:** Josh Li, to camera. **Length:** about 3 minutes (about 430 words at a natural pace).
**Goal:** one action, which is to click "Claim my data valuation" under the video.
**Rules:** no fake deals and no guaranteed numbers. Say "up to" and "typically". Disclose the referral fee.

That data doesn't sit on the internet. It sits inside businesses like yours.

And it's become one of the most valuable things a private business owns.

This has nothing to do with your customers' names, phone numbers or card details. They stay with you. It has everything to do with operational data: the workflows, the job history, the scheduling, the maintenance logs. All of it anonymised.

You don't sell equity. You don't give up control. Nothing changes in how you run the place on Monday morning. You're turning a record you already keep into cash.

And the reason the numbers are this big right now is simple. The budget is being deployed now. The businesses that move first get the premium prices. Once it's allocated, it's allocated.

And that's why we're doing this. We work with business owners every day, and this is the fastest money most of them will ever see from something they already have. Thirty days from now, one of two things will be true. Either your data is still sitting on a hard drive, doing nothing. Or you know exactly what it's worth, and maybe you've been paid for it.

## Recording notes
- **Open tight:** start mid-sentence on "This isn't for everyone." No intro and no logo sting. The first 5 seconds decide the play rate.
- **On-screen text, at the moments it's said:**
  - "$200K–$2M typical deal"
  - "Not customer data"
  - "No equity · no control given up"
  - "Claim my data valuation ↓"
- **Burn in captions.** Most people will watch on mute first.
- **Before recording:** confirm the partner name and committed figure in writing. Both are on screen and on the page.
- **Hosting:** upload to YouTube (unlisted), Vimeo or Loom. Paste the link into Funnel HQ → Integrations → Data → "Data page video". It shows up under the headline straight away.
