Every teaching point is a reason to buy, answering what and why, never a full how-to. Objections are handled inside the content. The stack is repeated with a reason behind each part. ## [11:00] The test every deal has to pass

[SLIDE: The 1.5x test]

If you picked financing, or you've been burned by a no money down pitch before, this is the part to watch closely.

Most no money down pitches fail for one reason. Someone piles debt onto a business that can't carry it. The payments come due, the business can't make them, and the buyer ends up feeding it from their own pocket.

There's one number that stops that from happening. Lenders call it the debt service coverage ratio. You take the yearly profit of the business you're buying, and divide it by the yearly payments on all the debt used to buy it.

[ON SCREEN: Yearly profit ÷ yearly debt payments. Target: about 1.5x.]

At about one and a half times, the business earns a dollar fifty for every dollar it owes. It pays its own debt and keeps a buffer for a bad month.
