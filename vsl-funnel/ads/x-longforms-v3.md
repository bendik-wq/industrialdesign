## V2 · Paying cash for a business

Paying cash for a business is the dumbest thing you can do with $2 million

Everyone who does it says the same sentence: "I didn't want any debt"

Congratulations. You emptied the account that was protecting your family, bought a company that is now the only thing between you and zero, and you paid more for it than the buyer who financed it, because the seller gave him better terms to get the deal done

Here's what the people who do this for a living do instead

They let the seller finance part or all of the price. The seller gets paid over time, out of the profit of the business he sold, and at a higher total price than most cash buyers will ever offer

They borrow the rest against the target's own cash flow, never their house

They test every deal the same way: the profit has to cover the repayments about one and a half times. If it doesn't, they walk

And the cash that would have bought one business sits in reserve and buys the next three

My first deal was 100% seller financed with a 9-month payment holiday. I didn't have $2 million. I had about a thousand cold calls and a board

The rich don't use their own money to buy businesses. They use the business's money

Own a profitable business doing $1M+ a year? DM me "STRUCTURE" before you write a cheque
