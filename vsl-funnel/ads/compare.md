# Opus vs DeepSeek: five belief-shift scripts

All three sets were written from the same brief (`ads/brief.md`, the four-sales method with all 17 elements) and the same house system prompt the app's AI writers use (`copyBrief('video')`). Files: `opus-scripts.md`, `deepseek-chat-scripts.md`, `deepseek-reasoner-scripts.md`. DeepSeek output is unedited.

## DeepSeek chat: claims that can't run as written
1. **Script 4:** "Josh built a multi seven-figure group after ten unprofitable-style mistakes cost other people their capital." Garbled. It ties Josh to the anonymous owner's losses.
2. **Script 1:** "the cash flow that took the seller twenty years to build" and "The business pays for itself while you sleep." Invented detail, and a promise of passive income.
3. **Scripts 1 and 3:** two "often" hedges the house rules ban.

## Lines worth stealing from DeepSeek chat
- "The ad account empties by the fifteenth of the month, and the queue gets longer than it was last year."
- "That voice is not wisdom. It is a memory." (on the fear of a second business)
- "You were the sales team, the HR department and the person who unblocked the toilet on a Sunday."
- "Your job changes from running the business to choosing which business to run."
- "The map stops being a list of markets to conquer and becomes a list of businesses to buy."

## Verdict
Run the Opus set. It's the only one that is both compliant and cinematic, with every ad its own story. DeepSeek chat has the best individual lines and the most raw energy, but it invents facts to get there, so it needs a human fact-check every time. That makes it a good source of lines, not a writer to ship from. The reasoner is safe and flat.
