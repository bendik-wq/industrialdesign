## 1. The Hidden Door
*Segment S1, owners stuck on a plateau. Controlling image: two doors in a long corridor.*

▸ CONTENT

Every owner you know is queuing at the same door.

A corridor. Two doors. One is crowded with owners and invoices. The other is unmarked.

There is a second door to growth almost nobody uses.

More ads raise the cost of the next customer. Another hire adds salary and management. A new agency sells the same promise. The second door adds customers, a team, and cash flow in one settlement.

That is the hidden way the biggest companies grow.

▸ HOPE

You built something real, and every path to grow it now runs through you.

Ad costs rose again this year. Payroll is heavier. The next customer costs more than the last.

That is organic growth. It is retail. You buy customers one at a time, at full price, with your cash and your hours.

The function is wholesale. You buy the whole business. Its customers, its team, its cash flow. The business pays for itself.

Commercial debt against the target's cash flow. Seller finance and seller rollover close the gap. The 1.5x cover test decides whether the business carries its own debt.

Listed deals are auctions. Banks reject structures, not buyers. Sellers sell to whoever makes them feel safe.

▸ VALUE

G&L is run by an operator who did it from nothing. Josh's first two deals were 100% seller financed. He built a multi seven figure group on them.

Every deal faces the 1.5x test before an offer. A deal that fails is walked away from.

The dream is a group that runs without you, and an exit at a group multiple. Small businesses trade at 3 to 5x EBITDA. Groups at 8 to 12x. Illustrative ranges.

You already know how to run one business. The second runs on structure, and it is easier than the first. One settlement beats years of retail growth.

Doing nothing has a price. A year of retail growth. Saturdays at the desk. The competitor who buys the business you wanted.

▸ IMPULSE

A deal that fails the cover test does not get done. Staying single is the bigger risk.

The deals that exist today go to whoever asks first, while owners are deciding. Most never list.

Wait, and the business you wanted is bought by someone else. A year passes on the plateau.

This is education, not legal, tax or financial advice.

For owners of profitable businesses doing $1M to $100M a year. If not, this is not for you yet.
