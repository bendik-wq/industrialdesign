What we collect

When you visit this site we record the pages you view, how you interact with them (including how much of our videos you watch), your approximate location derived from your IP address, your device and browser, and how you arrived (for example the ad or link you clicked). When you apply, we also collect the details and answers you give us.

Why

To run and improve this site, to assess whether we can help you, to contact you about your application by email, phone or WhatsApp, and to measure our advertising.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us operate: hosting (Cloudflare), email delivery, scheduling, analytics and advertising platforms (which may receive hashed contact details to measure ad performance). We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies

We use first-party cookies to recognise your browser and keep your application progress. Visitors in the EU/EEA are asked for consent before any data is shared with analytics or advertising platforms.

Your choices

Every email has an unsubscribe link. To access, correct or delete your information, reply to any of our emails.
