The buy box template

One page that pins down what you buy. Print it, fill it in, and send outreach only to owners inside it. A deal outside the box is a no, however good it sounds.

The business

Industrye.g. commercial HVAC, dental, accounting
What it sells, and to whome.g. maintenance contracts to commercial buildings
Why it fits what you already runshared customers, suppliers, staff or systems

The size

Revenue rangee.g. $2M to $6M a year
Cash flow range (EBITDA less capital spending)e.g. $400K to $1.2M a year
Most you would pay, as a multiple of cash flowillustrative: 3 to 5x for one small business

The place

Geographye.g. within two hours of your main site
Sites and locations you would take one.g. one or two

The owner

Owner profilee.g. founder, over 55, no successor named
Time the owner stays after the salee.g. 6 to 12 months of handover
Seller finance and seller rollover you will ask fore.g. 30% seller note, 10% rollover

The numbers it must pass

Debt service coverage1.5x or higher cash flow divided by all yearly loan payments
Customer concentratione.g. no customer over 20% of revenue
Financialsthree years, matching the tax returns

The walk-away lines

You walk away whenthe financials don't match the tax returns; the seller won't stay for the handover; the profit leaves with the owner; the structure can't reach 1.5x

Your outreach

Owners contacted each weeke.g. 25
Where the list comes fromindustry directories, associations, licence registers, your own suppliers
