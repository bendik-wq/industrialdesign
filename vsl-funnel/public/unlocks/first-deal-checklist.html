The first-deal checklist

Every step from the first owner conversation to a signed letter of intent, in order, with what to ask for at each one. Tick each step before you start the next.

  1. Write your buy box on one pageIndustry, size, geography, the owner profile and the walk-away lines. Every later step is checked against it.
  2. Build your owner listOwners inside the box, over 55, with no successor named. Directories, associations and licence registers are the first sources.
  3. Send the agreed outreach every weekThe letter, the email a week later, then the call. Keep a log of every contact and the date of the next one.
  4. Hold the first conversationListen for what the owner cares about after the sale: the staff, the customers, the name, their income. Write it down.
  5. Sign a mutual NDABefore any numbers change hands.
  6. Ask for three years of financialsStatements and tax returns. If they don't match, stop here.
  7. Run the 1.5x testYearly cash flow divided by all yearly loan payments. Use the deal calculator. Under 1.5x, change the structure or walk away.
  8. Agree the shape with the sellerCommercial debt, seller finance and seller rollover: how much of each, the seller note's term and rate, and how long the owner stays for the handover.
  9. Take that shape to two lendersLenders assess the structure and the target's cash flow. Bring the financials, the coverage and the seller's agreed terms.
  10. Send the letter of intentA signed, non-binding LOI with the price, the structure, the handover and the due diligence period. Have your lawyer read it before you send it.

A letter of intent is the start of due diligence, not a closed deal. Education only, not legal, tax or financial advice.
