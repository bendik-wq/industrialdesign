The No-Money-Down Acquisition Masterclass

  • Where the good deals are. Why the best acquisitions never get listed, and how owners find the business down the road whose owner wants out.

  • Whether it fits you. The numbers a business needs before an acquisition makes sense, so you can tell for yourself.
