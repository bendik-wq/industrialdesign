Before you apply

What counts as operational data?

Every job you've quoted, every booking, every invoice and every price you've charged over the years. It's the record of how your business runs, day to day.

Are my customers protected?

Yes. Everything is anonymised before it goes anywhere. The buyer wants to understand how the business operates, so your customers' details stay with you.

My business closed years ago. Does it still qualify?

Yes. Trading and closed businesses both get paid. If the records still exist on an old laptop, a server or a cloud account, they could be worth something.

Why now?

Rare, specialised data will hold its value. Most general business data is worth the most right now. Once buyers have enough of it, the price comes down.

Do I give up any ownership?

No. It's a new revenue line from something you already own, and you keep full ownership of your company.

What could I do with the money?

Pay down debt, make the hire you've been putting off, add working capital, or use it towards buying a competitor and recapitalising.
