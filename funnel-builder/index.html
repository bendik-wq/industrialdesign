What gets checked
  • Money first: a money gate at or above your revenue minimum sits before every sales step. Nothing reaches a call without passing it.
  • The gate fits the price: owners at your revenue minimum can easily afford the program (price no more than 4% of minimum revenue).
  • Unqualified people never get routed to a sales step.
  • Same promise everywhere: every customer-facing message uses the offer’s key terms, and the VSL uses all of them.
  • Same audience everywhere: the VSL and application name the revenue minimum.
  • Promise matches delivery: the delivery length equals the offer’s days.
  • Every link goes somewhere real, and every stage has an objective, a target and an owner.
  • Traffic covers the goal: channels send enough visitors for the monthly client goal.
