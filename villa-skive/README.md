# Villa Skive

A concept design for a large house on a granite headland on the Sunnmøre coast of Norway. *Skive* is Norwegian for disc.

- **Form:** a 64 m stone drum (a ring of rooms) around a 26 m winter garden under a glass dome.
- **Outside:** French 18th-century classical. Rusticated granite base, pilasters, cornices, a slate mansard with œil-de-bœuf windows, and a columned portico facing the land.
- **Two wings:** 16 m deep, they run 48 m east from the drum and end in square pavilions with tall French roofs. Together with the drum they enclose the cour d'honneur, the gravel entrance court with a fountain. The north wing holds the ballroom and guest apartments. The south wing holds the pool hall, the spa and staff flats.
- **Seaward side:** two cantilevered white concrete discs. The lower one carries a 22 m infinity pool and reaches 14 m past the cliff edge. The upper one is the master suite's terrace. Behind them, a 70° curtain wall of glass opens the Grand Salon.
- **Windows:** black steel with 600 mm square panes everywhere. The main French windows are 2.4 × 6.0 m.
- **Below ground:** the Lab, a rock-cut car and workshop level with a wine vault, plus an inclined lift down to a boathouse at sea level.

`index.html` contains the 3D massing model (three.js), the plan (A-101), the section (A-201), the window detail (A-501), the area schedule, the materials and the planning and engineering risks. Open it in a browser. It loads three.js from jsDelivr.

The 3D model uses the same dimensions as the drawings: floor levels at −3.5 / +4.5 / +13.0 / +20.5 / +25.5 m, 48 bays of 7.5° in the drum, and 4.2 m bays in the wings. Gross floor area is about 17 400 m².
