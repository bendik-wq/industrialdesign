Playbook

How it runs.

Long-form YouTube is the engine. Every video is filmed so it cuts into Reels. Built on Jeremy Haynes’ call-funnel practices and Daniel Iles’ short-form principles.

Read the VSL script
Content volume

Four films. Thirty-five Reels.

YouTube long-form
4/wk

The primary driver. One filming day.

Instagram Reels
5/day

35 a week, clipped from the long-form.

Clips per video
~9

So build 8–10 chapters into each video.

Film for the clip

Every chapter is a Reel.

Rules for every video

  1. One chapter answers one buyer question in 45–90 seconds.
  2. Open every chapter with a hook, as if it were the first 3 seconds of a Reel.
  3. Never say “like I said earlier.” Each chapter stands alone.
  4. One real number or deal example per chapter.
  5. Chapter titles on screen and in the description.
  6. Talk strategy to owners. No beginner tips.
  7. Topics come from real questions on calls, DMs and applications.
  8. Every video ends the same way: watch the VSL, apply.
Example · 9 chapters = 9 Reels

  9. What I’d buy if I owned your business.
